📰 Recent News: Dahej Trends 2024
UP / Bihar
Sarkari Naukri ke liye ₹20-40 Lakh Maange Ja Rahe Hain
Uttarpradesh aur Bihar mein government job holders ke liye dahej demand sabse zyada hoti hai — khaskar Police, Army aur Railway employees ke liye.
— Dainik Bhaskar Survey, 2024
Engineering
IT Engineer ₹10LPA+ par ₹15-25 Lakh Dahej Demand Common
Pune, Hyderabad aur Bangalore mein IT professionals ke liye dahej demand badhi hai. NRI engineer ke liye yeh figure ₹50 lakh tak pahunch jaati hai.
— NDTV Report, March 2024
Property
Zameen Wale Ghar ko Mila Sabse Zyada Dahej — MP Survey
Madhya Pradesh ke rural areas mein jinke paas 5+ acre zameen hai unhe ₹5-10 lakh extra dahej milta hai. Shahari plot wale bhi barabar benefit lete hain.
— Times of India, Jan 2024
Doctor
MBBS/MD Doctor ke Liye ₹30-75 Lakh Tak Demand
Medical profession sabse zyada dahej attract karta hai India mein. AIIMS doctor ke liye demand ₹1 Crore tak bhi record ki gayi hai kuch states mein.
— India Today, Feb 2024
Eklauta Beta
Only Son Hone par 35-50% Zyada Dahej Demand
Survey mein paya gaya ki families jahan sirf ek hi beta ho, wahan dahej demand average se 40% zyada hoti hai. Property inheritance ka factor kaam karta hai.
— Hindustan Times, 2024
NRI
NRI Rishton mein ₹50 Lakh – ₹2 Crore Tak Demand
USA, UK, Canada mein settled Indians ke liye dahej demand sabse zyada hai. Green Card ya citizenship hone par demand 3x ho jaati hai normal se.
— BBC Hindi, 2024
